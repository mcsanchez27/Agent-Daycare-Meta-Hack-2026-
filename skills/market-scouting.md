# Market Intelligence & Horizon Digesting

Playbook for monitoring ecosystem trends, framework updates, and technical innovations.

## Operational Standards
1. **High Signal Only:** Filter out noise and promotional fluff. Deliver dense, high-signal technical digests.
2. **Actionable Takeaways:** Connect external industry shifts directly to Mountain Man Studios architecture.
3. **Structured Briefs:** Organize digests into Scope, Breakthroughs, Risks, and Recommendations.
