# Notion Ticket Sync & Roadmap Management

Playbook for inspecting, syncing, and updating Notion tickets and batch task lists.

## Operational Standards
1. **Ticket Glances:** Surface status, priority, and checklists directly in-app without switching windows.
2. **Batch Progress:** Track flight completions and cross-reference automated tests against test checklists.
3. **Documentation:** Preserve ticket structure and leave clear consolidation summaries.
